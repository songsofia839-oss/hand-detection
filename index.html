HOW IT WORKS

From your hand
to a number.

01

Capture

Your webcam captures an image of your hand directly in the browser.

02

Detect

MediaPipe identifies the key landmarks across your hand and fingers.

03

Analyze

The detection algorithm analyzes the angles between your finger joints.

04

Recognize

The system determines how many fingers are open and displays the result.
